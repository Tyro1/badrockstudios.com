Click everything

Trees, rocks, ore veins, giant chests that fall from the sky. Hit them yourself and sweep the loot up with your cursor.

Hand the work over

Give each villager a job and step back. They chop, mine, gather and haul the loot home on their own, and every island you own keeps running while you're away on another.

Buy the next island

Every island brings new resources, new tools to forge and new trouble.

Clear the nests

Nobody knows what lives in the nests. Only that the creatures keep coming, and the purple keeps spreading, one island at a time. Gather your soldiers, pull the lever and tear the nest out at the root.

  • Active when you want, idle when you don't
  • No prestige, no resets: every island you buy stays yours
  • Skills that add new behaviours, not just bigger numbers
  • Puzzles, blessing shrines and villagers who want their lost things back

Questions

When does it come out?

Early Access on Steam, November 2026. A demo is on the way before that.

What can I play it on?

Windows PC, through Steam.

Which languages?

English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. Thai is on the way.

Is it idle or active?

Both. Click when you feel like it; your villagers keep working when you don't.

Will I ever have to reset?

No. There's no prestige. Every island you buy stays yours.

I found a bug. Where do I send it?

To support@badrockstudios.com. A screenshot helps a lot.

Does this website really break?

Yes. Don't worry, it all grows back.
